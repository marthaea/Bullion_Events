Meet Our CEO

Dear partners and clients, first and foremost, allow me to express my deepest gratitude to our dedicated team, whose exceptional talent, unwavering commitment, and passionate drive has enabled Bullion Events Limited to thrive through the years.

For over 5 years, we have spared no effort in planning, managing and executing event concepts to exceed our clients' expectations. Whether you are seeking a colorful function for your child's first birthday or a world-class stage featuring an international act, you will find all that you are looking for with us. We draw inspiration from our clients' needs and that is why every event is given our all, no matter how small.

Through our actions, we aspire to create lasting memories for families, corporate entities and diplomatic missions alike. Thank you for your trust — rest assured we will be there to accompany you at every step of your events journey.

Mercy Kansiime Chief Executive Officer
