Annual ONO YE MAAMA

Held annually Kampala, Uganda

A night of unmatched splendor and delight in honor of the role played by mothers across the country in raising their families and, in doing so, ensuring a legacy for all Ugandan people. Funds are raised each year in support of causes such as maternal mortality.

An end-of-year thanksgiving dinner that says "thank you" to loyal clientele, staff, patrons, friends and family — featuring people from the corporate world, government and NGOs, with entertainment, food and drinks.
